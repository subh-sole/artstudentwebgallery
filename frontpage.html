    ABOUT THE ARTIST

    Paniz is an Iranian artist currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Photography at Emily Carr University of Art and Design. Her practice mainly focuses on photography. Paniz's work greatly reflects her perspective on life and her surroundings. Her interest in psychology and human studies also influences the artist's practice in her recent works. In accordance with her personality, Paniz's style of photography is quiet and introverted. Through her art practice, Paniz is learning to enhance her senses to become more aware of her surroundings and to better understand others. With a diverse range of interests and a deep passion for exploring the human soul, Paniz's body of work encompasses street photography, self-portraits, and portraits. Street photography and industrial scapes, which focus on studying urban structures and environmental changes in human activities. In her earlier works, she was influenced by the Dusseldorf School of Photography in terms of compositions and framing. However, her recent projects lean more towards conceptual art, influenced by the work of Edward Burtynsky and the concept of the toxic sublime (finding beauty in something horrific). Other parts of her practice focus on portraiture and self-portraiture with the aim of studying the human soul and behaviors through culture and society. Paniz sees her everyday interactions and the people around her as exciting subjects to explore and observe. In this practice, she tries to see, not just look; to feel, not just touch; and to listen, not just hear, in order to have a deeper connection with her surrounding world. In this sense, her artwork has always been a reflection of her life experience and the journey of self-discovery.
